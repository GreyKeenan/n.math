There are two major ways to write out derivatives.
The first is by using Prime Notation, and the other is Leibniz Notation


Prime Notation
==================================================

Prime notation is simpler, more concise, and fairly intuitive.
However, it is less precise, and that can really matter in complex contexts.


Leibniz Notation
==================================================

Leibniz notation is a bit more verbose (especially in ASCII),
but it's very useful for keeping things straight in complex situations.

The major difference with Leibniz Notation is
that it specifies *what the derivative is with respect to*.
