* when entering intervals,
  infinity-ends should use non-inclusive brackets.
  inclusive (square) brackets will not be accepted as an answer. \
  `( -_-)`

* when listing intervals (and presumably sets) in answers,
  they **MUST** be joined with the "UOR" operator thing.
  You can't just list them with commas, or they'll be denied.
