we are given:

* the quadrant of the angle
* 1 of the trig funcs of the angle
  (not tan since that's easier)

we need to find other trig funcs from it

(calculator forbidden)


My previous approach was based on `t = s/c` & `ss = 1 - cc`.
That was a roundabout-way of doing it.
A more direct approach is based off of instead inferring the hypotenuse.

> see `./trigfuncs_from_point.md`


Problem 1
==================================================

	quad 4

	find sec & cot

	y = -2
	h = 3

	9 = 4 + xx
	5 = xx
	x = vT5

	sec = 3/vT5
	cot = vT5/-2

	( '-')b


	! I didn't confirm the quadrant!

Problem 2
==================================================

	quad 3
	cosA = -(7/10)
	find csc & tan

	x = -7
	h = 10

	100 = 49 + yy
	yy = 51

	WAIT!

		~~y = vT51~~

		~~csc = 10/vT51~~
		~~tan = -vT51/7~~

		almost got me

	y = (-)vT51
	
	csc = -10/vT51
	tan = vT51/7

	( '-')b


Problem 3
==================================================

Quadrant 4, `sinA = 12/13`.
Find sec & cot.

	y = 12
	h = 13

		13 * 3 = 39
		39 + 130 = 169

	169 = 144 + xx
	25 = xx
	+/- x = 5

		quad 4: -x

	sec = -13/5
	cot = -5/12

	( '-')b
