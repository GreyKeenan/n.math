Graphing
==================================================

when graphing a quadratic equation,
we convert it to a vertex-form parabola.
see "parabolas.md"

To do this, we "complete the square".
This means we are trying to convert the (axx + bx + d)
into a form where it can be factored into (x + y)(x + y) form.
To do that, we modify the equation by balancing the inside
of the parentheses with the opposite on the outside.
~~~
f(x) = 5xx + 30x + 1
f(x) = 5(xx + 6x) + 1

	(xx + 6x)
	take the 6: (6/2)^2 = 9
	insert the nine: 5(xx + 6x + 9)

	reduce that:
	(x + 3)^2

	then, since we are adding terms,
	we need to balance the equation again.
	Notice that we are adding (9 * 5) because its in the parentheses.
	So, we have to subtract 45
	f(x) = 5(x + 3)^2 + 1 + N45

f(x) = 5(x + 3)^2 + N44
~~~

And boom, that's vertex-form babeyei.
Our vertex is `(3, N44)`.
