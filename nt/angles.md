An angle is ...

There are a few ways to represent angles.

* degrees
* radians
* with another coordinate point


Degrees
==================================================

with an arbitrary value representing how far to turn.
90 degrees is a right angle,
180 is a full about,
and 360 is a full rotation back to the starting point.
Any degree modulo-ed by 360 is equivalent.


Radians
==================================================

with a value representing the distance traveled around
the unit circle to the endpoint.
Because the distance around the total circle is `2:pi:`,
radians are often represented with pi, such as `pi/4 = 45 degrees`.

Any radians modulo-ed by `2:pi:` is equivalent.


with another point
==================================================

Rather than defining the amount to turn,
just define the actual line that the angle is formed by.
If you have this information, it is often superior.


Converting between methods
==================================================

~~~
pi_radians = 180_degrees

tan(radians_or_degrees) = slope = y/x
tan(:pi:/4) = 1
~~~
